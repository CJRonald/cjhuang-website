Our paper **Triangular Fossa Cartilage Graft in Rhinoplasty: A Three-Dimensional Anatomical and Technical Feasibility Study** was accepted by [*Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*](https://doi.org/10.1097/PRS.0000000000012647){:target="_blank"} — a three-dimensional anatomical study validating the auricular triangular fossa as a donor site for structural grafting.
